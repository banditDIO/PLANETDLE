## Limits and disclosures
The game includes 21 selected objects, **not** every known moon or dwarf planet. Moon lists in the explorer show only objects in this atlas, not the complete moon systems. Relative sizes and orbit trajectories are diagrammatic, not ephemeris predictions. The canvas and profile portraits are scientifically *inspired* illustrations; they are **not accurate spacecraft photographs or 3-D topographical reconstructions**. Live textures are loaded for selected larger bodies from Solar System Scope if the user's browser can reach the provider, but they are not presented as NASA-verified raw spacecraft imagery. Venus uses a cloud-layer texture rather than a surface image; visible light cannot directly show its ground through the thick clouds. The orbital motion is deliberately accelerated for interaction. Atmospheric categories (None, Trace, Thin, Thick) and thermal categories are gameplay approximations, not scientifically exact classification standards; local temperatures vary greatly.
